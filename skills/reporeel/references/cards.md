# RepoReel Cards v0.6

Cards are shareable summaries generated from validated `audit.json`.

Rules: `audit.json` is source of truth; validate first; never reinterpret scores in renderers; card must say Launch Score/Launch Readiness; show status, category scores, and Reel Breaker; keep visual language clean and technical; prefer SVG.

Never auto-post. Generate assets the user may choose to share.
